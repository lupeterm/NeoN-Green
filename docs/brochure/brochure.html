What if we planted trees on our street – and took the bus? Find the hot spots, plant trees on the map, see the street – and how much cooler and cleaner it gets.

An interactive what-if tool for Heilbronn: it combines measurements from bike sensors, citizen weather stations and the city's tree inventory with a simple, explainable model of tree shade and cooling, and shows the result where people live – in the street.

1Find the hot spots

384 senseBox:bike rides (Jun–Oct 2026) reveal streets that are warmer than the rest of the ride – up to +0.7 °C.

2Plant trees on the map

Draw a stretch of street and plant trees. Shadows follow the sun; the panel shows the cooling, e.g. −3.7 °C on Weipertstraße.

3See it in the street

Look along Weipertstraße from the road centre, place trees, cars, the bus, motorbikes and bicycles, and read the effect.

How it works – honestly

  • Shade from each crown with the real sun position; evaporative cooling around trees.
  • Traffic: each vehicle = 200 trips a day, scaled from the traffic station by its emission factor.
  • Street views and cut-outs are AI images from our photos (open model, Apache 2.0).
  • A transparent teaching model – every number is editable – not a simulation.

Future work

From what-if estimates to CFD predictions

Today's numbers come from a simple model. In a street canyon, wind, buildings and trees decide where heat and pollutants go – that needs computational fluid dynamics.

  • CFD with OpenFOAM / NeoFOAM on GPU systems: resolve airflow around buildings, trees as porous drag, and heat and pollutant transport.
  • Same scenarios: export the trees and vehicles placed in the app as CFD cases for the street.
  • Validate against senseBox:bike and station measurements, then replace the estimates with physics-based predictions.
